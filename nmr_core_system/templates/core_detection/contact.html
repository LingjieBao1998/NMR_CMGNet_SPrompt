Ensemble Model (Recommended): Two mode of CMGNet + XGBoost models for comprehensive detection. Best for complex molecular structures and research applications.

XGBoost Model: Gradient boosting approach for faster processing. Suitable for quick analysis and simpler molecular patterns.

Standard Processing: 30-60 seconds per task
Large Datasets: May take 2-3 minutes depending on complexity
Processing Time: Varies by model selection (XGBoost is typically faster than CMGNet)

CSV Files: Two columns required (id, NMR) with comma-separated values in quotes. Maximum 5,000 entries per file.
Direct Input: 13C NMR data can be entered directly in the submission form as comma-separated chemical shift values.
Example: 176.49, 166.02, 153.61, 152.37, 148.32...

Automatic Download: Your task token file downloads automatically immediately after submission.
